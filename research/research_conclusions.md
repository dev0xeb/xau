## 2. Recommended Research Candidates for Systematization

### Recommended Strategy 1: Daily Session Open Bias Trend Follower (Candidate 3)
* **Why Promising**: Delivered **+$19,358.52 (+193.58% return)** over 3.5 years of out-of-sample data.
* **Evidence**: Aligns entries with institutional daily open momentum (Price > Daily Open = ONLY BUY).
* **Required Regime**: Strong Trend Expansion / Bullish & Bearish Trend Days.
* **Best Session**: London/NY Overlap (12:20 – 15:30 UTC).
* **Expected Frequency**: ~5 trades per week (1 trade / day).
* **Main Weakness**: Slower accumulation during tight mean-reverting chop weeks.
* **Next Step**: Deploy to MT5 automated live-execution environment.

### Recommended Strategy 2: Adaptive Market State Hybrid Engine (Candidate 5)
* **Why Promising**: Delivered **+$12,495.21 (+124.95% return)** with the **lowest Max Drawdown (-17.95%)**.
* **Evidence**: Dynamically routes signals to Daily Open Bias during trend days and to Session Sweep Reversals during range days.
* **Required Regime**: All regimes (Adaptive Trend & Range Dual State).
* **Best Session**: London/NY Overlap (12:20 – 15:30 UTC).
* **Expected Frequency**: ~5 trades per week.
* **Main Weakness**: Requires accurate real-time session expansion thresholding ($12.00 filter).
* **Next Step**: Deploy to automated portfolio manager.
